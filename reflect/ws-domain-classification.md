# reflection: the `ws:*` domain classification (the stream taxonomy's derivation)

**Provenance:** Operator-dispositioned 2026-07-09 (node #43), landed this session on
branch `process/ws-domain-classification`. The model was converged in two passes the
same day: a **riff→raff** convergence that produced three dispositions (Commons →
tag; `process` ⟂ `substrate` kept as two; dog-food reps → `ws:curriculum`), then two
**d-sense rulings** that closed the Sense (ruling A: split #5; ruling B: this
`reflect/` home + DYAD.md points, does not copy). It **refines #15** (which set node
identity via stream labels but left the taxonomy as free convention) and **applies
#21** (axis separation — the principle this cut instantiates). This note is the
durable single home: the taxonomy is re-derivable from here, not folklore.

## Problem (intent)

`ws:<stream>` labels are the grouping axis of the node ledger — the `Stream` field
of the Observe kanban (#13/#25), the column `bin/ws` renders. But the label set had
grown ad hoc (`substrate` / `process` / `curriculum`), with no rule that made a new
node's stream **decidable** rather than a matter of vibes. Two symptoms: `curriculum`
was silently carrying harness work (the telos-instrument #7, the enforcing checks
inside #5), and "Commons" kept pressing to become a fourth stream even though a
Commons-bound node is orthogonal to *which domain* it belongs to. The fix: derive the
stream axis from chiron's own **axis-separation** principle — give `ws:*` exactly one
concern, and route the others (reach, mode) onto their own axes.

## The principle applied: three orthogonal axes

`ws:*` is made to carry the **domain axis only**. Two other concerns that had been
leaking into stream labels are pulled onto separate axes, so no single symbol carries
two questions (axis separation, #21):

- **Domain** (`ws:*`) — *what kind of work is this?* The 2×2 below. One label per node.
- **Reach** (`dest:commons` tag) — *how far does it travel: chiron-local, or bound for
  Commons?* A separate label family. **Not** a stream, because every domain cell can
  have both a chiron-local and a Commons-bound member — Commons is not a fifth box.
  Pressure-tested 2026-07-09; rides as `dest:commons`, orthogonal to `ws:*`.
- **Mode** (unlabeled) — *build / KTLO / rep.* Off the `ws:*` axis entirely. KTLO is a
  non-domain (conceded); reps are the **run-mode of curriculum**, not a domain of their
  own. Deliberately carries no label.

**Why reach and mode are NOT domains.** A domain says what a node *is about*; reach
says how far it *travels*; mode says how it is *run*. Folding reach into the stream set
(a "Commons" stream) would force a false choice — a Commons-bound curriculum node would
have to be either curriculum *or* Commons — the exact conflation artifact #21 warns
against. Folding mode in (a "reps" stream) would split curriculum from its own
exercise. Each concern gets its own representation: `ws:*` for domain, `dest:*` for
reach, nothing for mode.

## The 2×2 (the domain axis)

The domain axis is itself a product of two sub-axes:

- **register** — content/model ⟂ machinery (*the thing being practiced* vs. *the
  apparatus that carries or measures it*).
- **subject** — product ⟂ self (*the learner-dyad's practice* vs. *chiron's own
  practice*).

|                       | **Product** (learner's practice) | **Self** (chiron's practice) |
|-----------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **content / model**   | `ws:curriculum`                  | `ws:process`                 |
| **machinery**         | `ws:harness`                     | `ws:substrate`               |

## The four discriminator questions (what makes a label decidable)

Each stream reduces to one question a node's intent either answers or does not:

- **`ws:curriculum`** — *what the learner works through?* (the content the learner-dyad
  practices)
- **`ws:harness`** — *delivers or measures the learner's practice?* (the instrument, not
  the content)
- **`ws:process`** — *how chiron's own dyad works?* (chiron's practice-model)
- **`ws:substrate`** — *model-agnostic runtime floor?* (the runtime that holds regardless
  of the practice model)

Curriculum ⟂ harness is the same content-⟂-machinery cut on the product side that
process ⟂ substrate is on the self side: the practice vs. the apparatus that runs it.

## The #5 split (ruling A — axis separation applied to our own ledger)

The new axis immediately earned its keep by exposing a conflation in our own ledger.
#5 was titled *"curriculum form + enforcing checks"* — one node carrying both a
content concern (the authoring **form**: rep-not-exposition, cited-taught, portability)
and a machinery concern (the **checks** that enforce it). Under the register cut those
are two cells: form = `ws:curriculum`, checks = `ws:harness`. So #5 was rescoped to the
form half (kept `ws:curriculum`), and the checks half was carved to a new node **#44**
(`ws:harness`) with a `Depends-on: #5` edge — the checks cannot be built until the form
is fixed. This is the repair-time face of #21 (dissolving an existing conflation),
recorded here as the taxonomy's first live proof.

## What this is not

A process-lesson (a *reflection*), not curriculum — it records how chiron classifies
its own work, and per the ontology (#7, single-home) DYAD.md #4 **points** here rather
than copying the model. The word "lesson" stays reserved for curriculum.
