# DIP Convergence — dyad-chiron working ledger

Codified lessons from the DIP session (2026-07-07). Unit: **turns-per-slot**
(operator↔agent exchanges to lock a slot). Goal: reuse accelerators, log
sinks, so later slots — and later sessions — converge cheaper. For this dyad
the ledger is doubly load-bearing: it is the seed specimen of the
reflect → extract-lesson → reuse loop our curriculum will teach.

## Accelerators (cut turns)

- **Destination before path.** The Operator's reorder — "lock the terminal
  state and summit first" — made the telos fall out as *the path to it*.
  Anchor the endpoint, then aim.
- **Craft-first informs the rest.** The Operator's opening insight (from DIP
  #1, dyad-aule): lock `craft` first; every later slot derived from it.
- **Study the reference dyad's process, not its output.** Reading aule's
  `retro/convergence-patterns.md` (how they converged) paid more than reading
  their anchor (what they converged on).
- **A locked constraint may re-level the craft.** Anchoring on "independent
  self-learning" surfaced the better craft (curriculum-as-artifact);
  re-locking was cheap and correct, not churn.
- **Derive names from locked slots.** The naming rubric (craft-fit, fade-fit,
  earnedness-fit, name-mechanics) fell straight out of the schema — candidates
  screened themselves.

## Turn-sinks (each was the agent's; each drew a correction)

- **Altitude slips.** Filled `craft_value` at the learner's level twice
  ("operator owns the loop" → the *dyad* does; "earned mastery" as an outcome
  property → the value ranges over *our practice*). Confirm a slot's altitude
  before filling it. (Aule's "scope error" lesson, rediscovered — it was in
  their ledger; should have been pre-loaded.)
- **Rule where the slot wanted the thing.** Framed a principle ("never trust
  apparent efficacy...") when `craft_value` wanted the cherished value.
  Extrapolate the value from the rule you're tempted to write.
- **Overstated consequence.** Argued to hold the birth commit from asserted
  irreversibility; reading the mechanism (`onboard.py`) refuted it. Verify a
  consequence against the mechanism before weighting a decision on it.
- **Unexamined mimicry.** Proposed `retro/` because aule had it. Derive names
  from inherited vocabulary and the craft's own collisions ("lesson" is our
  domain's most loaded word), don't copy a peer's coins.
- **Over-glossing a lock.** Locked phrases stay tight ("earnedness — outcomes
  earned, never conferred"); gloss homes elsewhere.
- **Scaffolding mistaken for slots.** "Tension" and "summit" were process
  locks that sequenced agreement, not identity — evaluate orthogonality before
  ingraining (tension = telos × value, folds into the invariant's gloss;
  summit decomposes into telos-terminal-state + acceptance check + registry
  entry-when-realized).

## Turns-per-slot (measured)

- craft: ~3 (spine given by Operator; one re-lock on the curriculum reframe)
- craft_telos: ~5 (summit reorder + unit test + reframe — the hard slot, as in
  aule)
- craft_value: ~4 (two altitude corrections + value-vs-principle)
- craft_invariant: ~2 (one plain-text rephrase)
- name: ~2 (rubric-driven elicitation)

Curve: 3 → 5 → 4 → 2. Sinks cluster where the agent proposed ahead of a
confirmed frame — aule's curve, same cause.

## The one rule (compounding insight, inherited and confirmed)

> **Elicit the spine and confirm the slot's scope/altitude first; propose
> wording only after both are pinned.** Every turn-sink this session was form
> proposed ahead of its spine — aule's chief lesson, independently re-earned
> here. Pre-load the reference dyad's ledger next time; a lesson reused is the
> whole point of the loop.
