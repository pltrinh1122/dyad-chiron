## 1. The two axes (ratified)

A curriculum topic is classified on **two orthogonal axes** — every topic carries a
coordinate on **both**, and conflating them is a category error (axis separation).

**LV — Level (provenance: *why* the topic is required).** Universal to every
curriculum topic.
- **pre-req** — must already hold to stand on G0; the curriculum screens for it,
  does not build it from zero (referral / future sub-G0 module).
- **L0 — dyad-core** — needed by *any* operator to run *any* dyad (e.g.
  falsification, the anti-capitulation floor).
- **L1 — craft-entailed** — imposed by *this dyad's specific craft* (e.g.
  curriculum-evaluation, because chiron *makes* curricula). Characterization: L1
  is what carries an operator from *operate-at-all* to *operate-efficiently*
  (toward the telos — minimize learning-turns), which makes L1 the telos-critical
  level.

**LY — Layer (operating decomposition: *where* in the dyad's operating structure the
topic lives).** Defined over dyad-operation topics; craft meta-skills that are not
SPAOR operating-phase skills take **LY = meta**.
- **Layer P** — the prerequisite floor (foundations to stand on G0).
- **Layer 0** — foundational operating knowledge (the dyad model, SPAOR, the
  interaction primitives, the channel model, provenance/substrate).
- **Layers 1–4** — the SPAOR phases: **1** Sense · **2** Plan/Act · **3** Observe ·
  **4** Reflect (loop-ownership).
- **cross-cutting / meta** — dispositions that span phases; craft meta-skills.

**The named hazard:** *"Layer 0"* (an LY value — foundational operating knowledge) is
**not** *"L0"* (an LV value — dyad-core provenance). Same numeral, orthogonal axes.

**Soft edge held for disposition (honest, not smoothed):** LV has two readings the
session used that can diverge — **provenance** (L0 = any dyad · L1 = this craft) vs
**threshold** (L0 = operate-at-all · L1 = operate-efficiently). They agree on most
topics but split on **loop-ownership**: it is universal to *every* dyad (→ L0 by
provenance) yet is the improvement/efficiency skill (→ L1 by threshold). The **two-axis
structure and the LY/LV vocabulary are ratified**; sharpening the LV cut — provenance,
threshold, or LV itself splitting into two sub-axes — is the one open disposition,
non-blocking. (Same shape as the logic/analytics prereq↔taught seam: a level "or" is
often two-things-at-two-coordinates.)
