## 1. Scope and the setup↔operation seam

This maps the skills to **operate** a dyad — carry a *formed* dyad standing on
G0 from novice to **graduation = coach-free practice** (the anchor's
coach-absent test: the dyad owns its improvement loop, cost-per-job falling
coach-absent). It is distinct from the dyad-**setup** curriculum (#8–#11),
which carries a *pre-dyad* across the prerequisite floor to *standing on G0*.
The two meet at the **P↔0 seam** (§2): setup ends where operation begins.

## 2. The layered skill inventory

Tagged **K** (knowledge, sealed-key testable) / **P** (practice, earned by
reps) and analytic↔tacit. Grounded in this dyad's own model (SPAOR, the
interaction primitives, the invariants) and its ledger.

- **Layer P — prerequisites to stand on G0** *(the floor beneath operation; §3–§4)*.
  Below this a person is a *pre-dyad*, not a novice-operator — setup territory
  (#8–#11).
- **Layer 0 — foundational operating knowledge** *(K · analytic)*: the dyad
  model · SPAOR · interaction primitives (`direct/steer/ratify/falsify` ∥
  `propose/report/elicit/deliver`, `d-*`, `riff/raff`) · the two-channel model
  · provenance + substrate.
- **Layer 1 — Sense** *(P · mostly tacit)*: intent articulation (intent+invariant
  form) · molten-vs-settled detection · invariant elicitation · interpretation-
  falsification before action · the axis-separation reflex.
- **Layer 2 — Plan/Act** *(K+P · analytic-leaning)*: decomposition · disposition
  scope (rule intent+invariants, delegate mechanism) · reversibility judgment ·
  delegation/provisioning · autonomy-boundary calibration.
- **Layer 3 — Observe** *(P · perceptual/tacit)*: high-bandwidth view-reading ·
  falsify-before-trust (check the target, not the description) · drift/gap
  detection (a manual patch is a gap signal).
- **Layer 4 — Reflect** *(the graduation-defining meta-skill · most tacit)*:
  extract-the-right-lesson · reclassify-then-fix · honest-verdict/no-miss ·
  codify-in-substrate · self-falsification. **This is loop-ownership; owning it
  coach-absent is graduation.**
- **Cross-cutting dispositions** *(P/tacit)*: trust calibration · turn economy ·
  earnedness/no-conferral · anti-sycophancy (the covalent SH move) · provenance
  reflex.

**Leverage ≠ frequency (rep 3):** analytic *knowledge* is frequent (Layer 0 is
fact-dense) but leverage concentrates in the tacit region (Layers 1/3/4 + the
dispositions), and the graduation-defining layer (Reflect) is the most tacit.

## 3. The Operator G0 K-prerequisites — two orthogonal axes

**Cognitive foundations ⟂ operational access** — a complete K-screen needs
both (axis separation).

**Axis I — cognitive foundations** (Operator conjecture, 2026-07-11):
1. **Foundational logic** — implication, necessary/sufficient, validity. The
   deepest floor; below Layer-0's proto-grounding.
2. **Analytics** — correlation vs causation, confounds, evidence. *(Straddles
   the prereq↔taught seam: ε-analytics is a prereq; mastery-analytics is #61's
   output. Screen the ε floor only.)*
3. **Systems-thinking** — decomposition, boundaries/interfaces, feedback;
   **contains axis-separation**, this dyad's own design principle.
4. **Systems (MVC, compute/persistence types)** — *domain-conditional, not
   foundational*: a prereq for *technical-domain* dyads only; filed under
   domain-baseline, not the universal four (else it re-narrows the
   domain-general craft, rep 3/r6).
5. **LLM-agent model** — inferencing, context, predisposition. Sharpens
   "model-of-the-Agent"; the whole falsify-before-trust discipline presupposes
   it.
- *Candidate gap (probe):* **probability/uncertainty reasoning** (base rates,
  calibration) — load-bearing for the (K,P)/confidence machinery, not cleanly
  inside the five.
- *Partial order:* `logic ⊂ analytics ⊂ systems-thinking`; (4) and (5) hang
  more independently.

**Axis II — operational access** (from the earlier K-set): medium literacy ·
proto-grounding · proto-decomposition · domain-adjacent baseline · tool/
substrate literacy. The "can transact with the substrate at all" floor.

## 4. The Operator G0 pre-req assessment

A **screening instrument administered before G0** — the gate to the gate, not
a lesson. Per prereq, **three outcomes** driving **one routing decision**
(decision-first, `reflect/decision-architecture.md`): *route this operator →
{admit · redirect-external · defer-to-future-sub-G0}.*

- **MEETS** (≥ ε) → admit toward G0.
- **GAP-TEACHABLE** → **[SUB-G0]**: build a future sub-G0 module (this is the
  "reduce the prereq below G0" route); external stopgap interim.
- **GAP-REFERRAL** → **[EXT]**: acquire externally; curriculum won't/can't
  build it (or screen out).

**K and P screen differently** (r2/r4): K prereqs → sealed-key items (the
five in §3-I are cleanly item-able — a real standardized battery). P/disposition
prereqs → noisy behavioral probes, so **screen generously — reject only on
zero, admit any nonzero ε** (P2 trust-verify-seed and P4 iteration-tolerance
are the ur-forms the curriculum grows; a low score is its *input*, not a
rejection). Over-screening P is conferral-in-reverse.

**Two symmetric conferral failures to avoid:** false-admit (telling an under-ε
operator "you're ready" → they fail G0) and false-referral (denying an ε+
operator → burning a real one). The screen must earn its verdict both ways.

**Scope frontier.** The [SUB-G0] set is the **future-scope backlog**, ordered
by **population-unlocked ÷ build-cost**. Falsifiable first pull-in:
**LLM-agent model** (chiron-adjacent, teachable, most-load-bearing, commonly
absent). Logic/analytics/systems-thinking stay **[EXT]** general-education;
"Systems" stays **[EXT/domain]**.

## 5. The substrate-competence model

**Capitulation = `no-self-ratify` at the substrate level.** If the Operator
can't evaluate a substrate change, the Agent self-ratifies it — earnedness
leaks at the layer where the Agent's competence advantage is largest (the
steepest trust-gradient). The conflated "understand the substrate" separates
into three competences: **judge ⟂ diagnose ⟂ author.**

- **Author/evolve — NOT required of the Operator.** Delegable to the Agent-half
  (the fifth invariant on the substrate object) or escalatable to substrate
  experts. *Existence proof:* this very session — the Operator evolved the
  curriculum-substrate by judging+directing+falsifying, authoring none of it,
  and it was not capitulation *because the falsification was real*.
- **Judge — the non-delegable floor.** Falsify a proposed change; dispose
  intent+invariants. **Judge-without-author ≠ capitulation iff judgment is
  competent** — so the anti-capitulation skill *is falsification* (#61 aimed at
  the substrate object; loop closure).
- **Diagnose-to-report — the triage floor.** Notice + localize + describe;
  escalation path already exists (`bin/ws intake`, the inbound dyad-to-dyad
  falsification request).

**Self-verifying substrate is anti-capitulation infrastructure.** `criteria/`
checks carry *mechanism*-verification (is the bash correct?), leaving the
Operator to judge only *intent*-fitness (does it do what we meant?) — so
`./check`/CI coverage directly lowers the Operator's substrate bar. But it does
not reach zero: the Operator must still **falsify the checks' *severity***
(this session's emptiness-assert passed while wrong — a non-severe probe caught
only by falsification). The Operator audits the *verifiers*, not everything
they verify.

**Ratified dispositions (Operator, `raff:` 2026-07-11):**
- **(a) Audit-the-verifiers does not recurse infinitely; the prereq is the
  floor.** Falsifying a check's *severity* is a fixed, prereq-level reasoning
  act (would this observation differ if the claim were false?) — the axis-B
  severe-probe skill applied to the check. It needs no meta-meta-check; the
  human's foundational reasoning (§3-I) is the base case. **The
  substrate-competence floor = the G0 prerequisite floor** — they are the same
  floor.
- **(b) Tiering across authoring vs. using is ratified.** The substrate-
  competence *ceiling* is a per-dyad parameter (parameter, not scope — r6):
  a **substrate-authoring dyad** (chiron) holds judge+diagnose in the Operator
  and author in the Agent-half — all three tiers in-dyad; a **substrate-using
  dyad** holds judge+triage in the Operator and escalates authoring to a
  substrate-expert tier (the Commons form, a substrate-authoring dyad). The
  **floor (judge + diagnose-to-locus) is universal; the ceiling is tiered.**

## 6. Novice → mastery → graduation (Fitts-Posner mapping)

- **Novice** — holds Layer 0; runs SPAOR haltingly under heavy coach
  scaffolding (cognitive/declarative stage).
- **Journeyman** — Layers 1–3 fluent; falsification and axis-separation gone
  reflex (the ledger logs this transition across sessions: associative→
  autonomous).
- **Mastery** — Layer 4 self-sustaining; the dyad owns its improvement loop.
- **Graduation = Layer 4 running coach-free**, cost-per-job trending down — the
  anchor's coach-absent test. Per the claim-strength ladder
  (`falsification-curriculum.md` §5): this certifies transfer-readiness;
  mastery is the post-graduation job-record.
